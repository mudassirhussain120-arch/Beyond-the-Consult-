PSA prostate se banne wala protein hai, jise aam khoon ke test se napa jata hai. PSA zyada aane ka matlab cancer nahi hota, aur normal aane se cancer poori tarah khatam bhi nahi hota. Yeh sirf ek maloomat hai jo aap ki alamaat aur muayene ke saath dekhi jati hai. Sirf number dekh kar andaza lagane ke bajaye poochhein ke nateeja kaun aur kab samjhayega.

PSA is a protein made by the prostate, and a simple blood test measures it. A raised result does not mean you have cancer, and a normal result does not rule it out, so it is one piece of information to be read together with your symptoms and examination. Please ask who will explain your result and when, rather than guessing from the number alone.
