A varicocele is a swelling of the veins around the testicle, a bit like varicose veins in the leg. It is common and often harmless, and many men have no symptoms at all. For some it can cause discomfort or affect fertility, so it is worth getting checked.

Swipe for the signs to look out for in English and Roman Urdu ➡️

Go to a hospital emergency department straight away if: you have sudden, severe pain in a testicle, with or without nausea or vomiting.
