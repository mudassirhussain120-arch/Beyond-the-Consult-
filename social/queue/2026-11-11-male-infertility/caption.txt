Male infertility means a couple has not been able to conceive and the man's sperm may be part of the reason. In many couples the man is part of the reason, alone or together with his partner. It is a medical condition, not a weakness, and it is not anyone's fault. If you have been trying for about a year without success, it is sensible for both of you to be checked together.
