Testicular cancer is when cells in a testicle grow out of control and form a lump. It has a good outlook and is very often curable, but the key is to be seen early. The most common sign is a lump or swelling in a testicle, often painless. Painless is not the same as harmless. It is not caused by anything you did, and embarrassment should never stop you from being checked.

See a doctor within days if you notice a new lump or change in a testicle, even if it does not hurt. Go to a hospital emergency department straight away if: you have sudden, severe pain in a testicle, with or without nausea or vomiting.

Testicular cancer tab hota hai jab testicle ke cells be-qaabu barhte hain aur ganth ban jati hai. Iska nateeja aksar achha hota hai aur yeh bohat dafa theek ho jata hai, lekin zaroori hai ke jaldi doctor ko dikhayein. Sab se aam nishani testicle mein ganth ya soojan hai, jo aksar dard ke baghair hoti hai. Dard na hona matlab nuqsan na hona nahi. Yeh aap ke kisi kaam se nahi hota, aur sharm ko jaanch se na rokein.

Testicle mein nayi ganth ya tabdeeli dikhe, chahe dard na ho, to chand din ke andar doctor ko dikhayein. Achanak shadeed dard ho to foran emergency jayein.
