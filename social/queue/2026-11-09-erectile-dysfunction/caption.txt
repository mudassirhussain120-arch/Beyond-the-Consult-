Erectile dysfunction (ED) means often having trouble getting an erection, or keeping it firm enough for sex. An off night now and then is normal. It becomes a medical concern when it keeps happening. It is common, it is not your fault, and it can be an early sign of other health problems, so it is worth getting checked.
