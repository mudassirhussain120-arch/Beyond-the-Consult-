A urinary catheter is a thin, soft tube that drains urine from the bladder into a bag. The most important habits are washing your hands before and after touching it, keeping the bag below the level of the bladder, and keeping the tube free of kinks. Please ask your team how long it is meant to stay in and how often it will be changed.

Go to a hospital emergency department straight away if: the catheter stops draining with a painful full bladder, or fever, or heavy bleeding.
