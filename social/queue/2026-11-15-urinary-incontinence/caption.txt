Urinary incontinence means leaking urine when you do not mean to. It can be a few drops when you cough, or a sudden flood before you reach the toilet. It is common, it is not your fault, and in most people it can be improved or treated. Many people suffer for years without telling anyone because of embarrassment. It is not a normal part of having children or growing old, and help is available.
