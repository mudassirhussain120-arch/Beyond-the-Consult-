Flexible cystoscopy mein urologist aik patli, mudne wali camera se masane ke andar seedha dekhta hai. Pehle numbing gel lagaya jata hai aur test aam taur par mukhtasar hota hai. Baad mein thori der jalan, baar baar peshab aur peshab mein thora khoon ho sakta hai. Khoob pani peena madad karta hai, siwaye iske ke doctor ne mana kiya ho.

A flexible cystoscopy lets your urologist look directly at the inside of the bladder using a thin, bendable camera. A numbing gel is used first, and the test is usually short. Stinging, passing urine more often and a little blood in the urine can follow for a short time. Drinking plenty of fluids helps, unless you have been told to limit them.

Go to a hospital emergency department straight away if: fever or shivering, you cannot pass urine, or heavy bleeding with clots.
