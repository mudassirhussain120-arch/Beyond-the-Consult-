Vasectomy aik chhota operation hai jo sperm le jane wali nalion ko band kar deta hai. Yeh un mardon ke liye hai, aam taur par apni biwi ke saath mashwarey se, jo yaqeen se mazeed bachay nahi chahte, aur ise ulatna aasan nahi. Neel aur sooj aam taur par hafte do hafte mein theek ho jati hai. Baad mein semen test se tasdeeq hoti hai, is liye nateeje se pehle is par bharosa na karein.

A vasectomy is a small operation that blocks the tubes carrying sperm. It is meant for men, usually together with their partner, who are sure they want no more children, and it is not easy to reverse. Bruising and swelling usually settle within a week or two. A semen test afterwards confirms it has worked, so please do not rely on it until you have the result.
