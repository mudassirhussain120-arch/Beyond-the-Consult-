DJ stent ek patli, naram nali hai jo ureter mein lagayi jati hai taake gurda peshab nikal sake. Yeh aarzi hoti hai. Bohat se logon ko pehlu mein dard, baar baar peshab ya halka gulabi peshab hota hai. Yeh aam hai aur aksar kisi masle ki nishani nahi. Zaroori baat yeh hai ke stent waqt par nikalwayi ya badli jaye, is liye ghar jane se pehle tareekh zaroor poochh lein.

A DJ stent is a thin, soft tube placed in the ureter so the kidney can drain. It is temporary, and many people feel an ache in the side, pass urine more often, or notice pink urine while it is in. This is common and usually not a sign of a problem. What matters is getting it removed or changed on time, so please ask for the date before you go home.

Swipe for what is normal in English and Roman Urdu ➡️

Go to a hospital emergency department straight away if: fever or shivering, severe side pain with vomiting, or you cannot pass urine.
