TURP mein barhe hue prostate ka woh hissa nikala jata hai jo peshab ka rasta rok raha hota hai. Operation ke baad catheter aik do din rehta hai, aur peshab shuru mein gulabi ya surkh hota hai phir saaf ho jata hai. Jalan aur baar baar peshab kuch hafton tak reh sakta hai, aur dry ejaculation aam hai. Apni team se poochhein ke kya umeed rakhein aur follow-up kab hoga.

TURP trims away part of an enlarged prostate that is blocking the flow of urine. After the operation a catheter stays in for a day or two, and the urine is pink or red at first before it clears. Burning and passing urine often can last some weeks, and dry ejaculation is common. Please ask your team what to expect and when you will be reviewed.

Go to a hospital emergency department straight away if: you cannot pass urine, fever or shivering, or heavy bleeding with clots.
