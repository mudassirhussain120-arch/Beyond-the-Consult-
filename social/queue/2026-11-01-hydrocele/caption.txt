A hydrocele is a collection of fluid around the testicle. It is usually painless and harmless, but any swelling in the scrotum should be checked by a urologist so you know exactly what it is.

Go to a hospital emergency department straight away if: you have sudden, severe pain in a testicle, or a swelling that quickly becomes hard or painful.
