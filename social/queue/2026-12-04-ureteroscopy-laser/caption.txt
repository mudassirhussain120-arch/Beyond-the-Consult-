Ureteroscopy with laser mein gurde ya ureter ki pathri ka ilaaj peshab ki nali ke zariye hota hai, is liye jild par koi cut nahi lagta. Aap behosh hote hain, patli telescope pathri tak jati hai, aur laser usay tukron mein tor deta hai jo nikale jate hain ya peshab ke saath nikal jate hain. Kuch dair ke liye stent lag sakta hai. Ghar jane se pehle poochh lein ke stent kab nikalna hai.

Ureteroscopy with laser treats a kidney or ureter stone through the urine tube, so there is no cut on the skin. You are asleep, a thin telescope reaches the stone, and a laser breaks it into pieces that are removed or pass in the urine. A stent may be left in for a while. Please ask for the date it needs to come out before you leave.

Go to a hospital emergency department straight away if: fever or shivering, severe side pain with vomiting, or you cannot pass urine.
