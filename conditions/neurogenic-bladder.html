Condition guide

Neurogenic bladder (bladder problems from nerve damage)Neurogenic bladder (nason ke nuqsan se bladder ke masail)

A neurogenic bladder is a bladder that does not work normally because of nerve damage, for example after a spinal injury, stroke or spina bifida. Protecting the kidneys is the main goal. This guide is written for patients, families and carers. It explains the causes, the tests, the treatment options, and the warning signs that need urgent help.

Neurogenic bladder woh bladder hai jo nason ke nuqsan ki wajah se aam tarah kaam nahi karta, maslan reerh ki chot, stroke ya spina bifida ke baad. Kidneys ki hifazat bunyadi maqsad hai. Yeh guide marizon, ghar walon aur dekh bhaal karne walon ke liye hai. Yeh wajohat, tests, ilaaj ke intikhabat, aur fauran madad ki nishaniyan samjhati hai.

Neurogenic bladder kya hai

Bladder ko woh nasein control karti hain jo bladder, reerh ki haddi ki rassi aur dimaagh ke darmiyan chalti hain. Yeh bataati hain ke bladder bhar raha hai aur aap ko sahi waqt par khali karne deti hain. In nason ko nuqsan ho to bladder theek se khali nahi hota, ghalat waqt par sikarta hai, ya dono. Doctors ise neurogenic bladder, ya neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction kehte hain.

Is se peshab rissna, peshab karne mein mushkil, baar baar peshab ke infection, aur sab se ahem, agar dekh bhaal na ho to kidneys ko nuqsan ho sakta hai. Achi baat yeh hai ke sahi plan ke saath zyada tar log theek rehte hain. Yeh guide batati hai ke yeh kyun hota hai, kidneys kyun ahem hain, bunyadi ilaaj kaun se hain, aur fauran madad ki nishaniyan kya hain. Yeh is masle ke shikaar logon aur un ke ghar walon aur dekh bhaal karne walon ke liye hai.

Neurogenic bladder ko maahir ke saath banaye gaye plan aur aap ke baqaida mamool ki zaroorat hai. Meherbani karke alamaat ke zaahir hone ka intezar na karein, urologist ko dikhayein.

Is ki wajohat

Koi bhi masla jo dimaagh, reerh ki rassi ya bladder ki nason ko nuqsan pohanchaye, yeh paida kar sakta hai. Aam wajohat:

  • Reerh ki rassi ki chot, maslan sarak ke hadse ya oonchai se girne se.
  • Stroke ya dimaagh ki chot.
  • Spina bifida aur doosre paidaishi masail (dekhein vesicoureteric reflux guide).
  • Multiple sclerosis, Parkinson ki bimari aur nason ki doosri bimariyan.
  • Diabetes, jo kai saalon mein nasein kharab kar sakti hai.
  • Pelvis ka operation ya radiotherapy, jo bladder ke qareeb nasein zakhmi kar sakta hai.
  • Disc ka khisakna, reerh ki haddi ka tumour, ya infection jo reerh ki rassi par dabao dale.

Bladder par kaise asar hota hai

Nasein jahan zakhmi hon us ke hisab se bladder mukhtalif tareeqon se kaam kar sakta hai, aur kuch logon mein mila julaa asar hota hai:

  • Overactive bladder jo achanak aur zor se sikarta hai, achanak hajat aur rissne ke saath (dekhein overactive bladder guide).
  • Underactive bladder jo theek se nahi sikarta, is liye bohat bharta hai aur khali nahi hota. Bladder bohat bhar jata hai aur tapak sakta hai (dekhein urinary retention guide).
  • Nikasi jo dheeli nahi hoti jab bladder sikarta hai, is liye bladder band darwaze ke khilaf sikarta hai aur dabao barhta jata hai.

Yeh tarz tests se pata chalta hai. Yeh ilaaj tay karta hai, is liye meherbani karke theek jaanch ke baghair goliyan ya catheter ka mamool shuru na karein.

Yeh kyun ahem hai: kidneys

Jab bladder khali nahi hota ya zyada dabao mein rehta hai to dabao aur peeche rehne wala peshab kidneys ki taraf wapis ja kar waqt ke saath unhein nuqsan pohancha sakta hai, aksar alamaat ke baghair. Baar baar ke infection aur pathri khatra barha dete hain. Is liye ilaaj ka bunyadi maqsad bladder ke andar dabao kam rakhna aur isay theek se khali karte rehna hai, taake kidneys sehatmand rahein, aur phir aap ko khushk aur aaramdeh rakhna.

Aam nishaniyan

Nishaniyan bohat mukhtalif hoti hain, aur kuch logon ko lagbhag kuch mehsoos nahi hota. In mein yeh shamil ho sakti hain:

  • Peshab rissna, hajat ke saath ya us ke baghair.
  • Peshab na kar paana ya sirf thora karna, ya kamzor dhaar.
  • Bladder bharne ka ehsaas na hona, ya mukhtalif ehsaas jaise paseena, sar mein dhamak ya pait mein takleef.
  • Baar baar peshab ke infection, ya bagair kisi aur wajah ke bukhar.
  • Dhundla ya badboodar peshab, ya peshab mein khoon.
  • Bladder ya kidney mein pathri.

Agar aap ko reerh ki chot ya nason ki bimari hai to meherbani karke sirf ehsaas par bharosa na karein. Baqaida jaanch ahem hai kyunke kidney ka nuqsan khamosh ho sakta hai.

Jaanch kaise hoti hai

Urologist, aksar rehabilitation ke maahir ke saath, aam tor par:

  • Nason ke masle ke baare mein poochhenge, bladder aur aanton ki aadaat, rissna, infection aur dawayein.
  • Muaina karenge aur aap ki nasein aur jild dekhenge.
  • Bladder diary rakhne ko kahenge, aur ho sakta hai ke aap se kahein ke kitna peshab karte hain woh likhein.
  • Peshab aur khoon ke tests karenge, jin mein yeh jaanch shamil hai ke kidneys kitni acha kaam kar rahe hain.
  • Kidneys aur bladder ka ultrasound karenge.
  • Khaas pressure tests (urodynamics) karenge. Aik patli nali bladder bharti hai jabke dabao record hota hai. Is se pata chalta hai ke bladder kaise kaam karta hai, aur yeh ilaaj ki planning ka aham test hai.
  • Kabhi kabhi bladder ke andar camera se dekhenge, ya kidney scan karwayenge.

Ilaaj ke tareeqay

Ilaaj har shakhs ke liye alag hota hai. Aam maqasid kidneys ki hifazat, infection se bachna, aur aap ko khushk rakhna hain. Intikhabat mein yeh shamil hain:

Clean intermittent catheterisation (CIC)

Din mein kai baar bladder ko poori tarah khali karne ke liye saaf patli nali dalna. Yeh sab se zyada istemal hone wala aur aksar behtareen tareeqa hai, kyunke yeh bladder poora khali karta hai, kidneys ko bachata hai, aur mustaqil nali se bachata hai. Zyada tar log sikh sakte hain, ya tarbiyat ke baad dekh bhaal karne wala kar sakta hai. Is ke liye saaf haath aur saaf saman aur baqaida mamool zaroori hai.

Taweel muddat ka catheter

Aisi nali jo andar rehti hai, ya urethra se ya pait ki deewar se (suprapubic catheter). Yeh tab istemal hoti hai jab intermittent catheterisation mumkin na ho. Is mein infection, nali band hone aur pathri ka khatra zyada hota hai, aur baqaida badalna aur dekh bhaal chahiye.

Bladder ko dheela karne wali dawayein

Jo bladder zyada sikarta hai us ke liye dawayein dabao kam kar sakti hain aur rissna ghata sakti hain. In ke ased ho sakte hain jaise munh khushk hona aur qabz, aur aksar catheterisation ke saath istemal hoti hain.

Bladder mein botulinum toxin ke injections

Aisa injection jo bladder ke muscle ko kai mahine ke liye dheela karta hai, phir dobara lagana parta hai. Bohat se logon ko is ke baad catheter istemal karna parta hai.

Operation

Un chand logon ke liye jin ka bladder doosre ilaaj ke bawajood zyada dabao mein rehta hai, operation bladder bara kar sakta hai ya peshab ka rasta badal sakta hai. Nikasi ka operation, ya catheter ke liye pait ki deewar tak rasta banana bhi madad kar sakta hai. Yeh maahir ke operation hain.

Sahi ilaaj bladder ki qism, aap ke haathon aur tarz-e-zindagi par munhasir hai. Catheter ka mamool tab acha kaam karta hai jab theek tarbiyat se shuru ho. Meherbani karke kisi aur ka mamool nakal na karein.

Peshab ke infection aur pathri

Neurogenic bladder mein peshab ke infection aam hain, khaas tor par catheter ke saath. Har musbat peshab test ke ilaaj ki zaroorat nahi hoti, lekin bukhar, naya rissna, naya dard, peshab ki boo ya rang mein tabdeeli, ya tabiyat kharab hona aise infection ki nishani ho sakti hai jis ka ilaaj zaroori hai. Antibiotics sirf doctor ke mashwaray se aur poora course lein. Kaafi peena, bladder ko poori tarah aur baqaidgi se khali karna, aur saaf mamool infection kam karte hain. Pathri bhi aasani se ban sakti hai, is liye doctor se jaanch ke baare mein poochein. Dekhein urine infection guide.

Aanten, jild aur rozmarra

Nason ka nuqsan aksar aanton ko bhi mutasir karta hai, aur qabz bladder ke masail barha deta hai, is liye aanton ka mamool plan ka hissa hai. Apni jild ka khayal rakhein, khaas tor par agar aap der tak baithte ya letay hain, aur isay saaf aur khushk rakhein taake zakhm na banein. Din bhar kaafi pani peene ki koshish karein. Pads aur madadgar cheezein ilaaj ke dauran madad kar sakti hain. Rehabilitation team, occupational therapist aur aisa ghar wala jo mamool samajhta ho bara farq daal sakte hain. Neurogenic bladder wale bohat se log kaam karte hain, parhte hain, shadi karte hain, safar karte hain aur bharpoor zindagi guzarte hain.

Umar bhar follow-up

Neurogenic bladder ko umar bhar follow-up chahiye, kyunke bladder waqt ke saath badal sakta hai aur kidney ka nuqsan khamosh ho sakta hai. Aap ke doctor aam tor par kidney aur bladder ke scans, kidney ke khoon ke tests aur kabhi kabhi pressure tests dohrayenge, aksar saal mein aik baar ya jaisa mashwara ho. Meherbani karke theek mehsoos karne par bhi yeh visits na chhorein, aur apni tamam reports saath layein.

Fauran madad kab lein

Agar in mein se koi bhi ho to fauran apne qareebi hospital ke emergency department jayein:

  • Agar aap ko reerh ki rassi ki chot hai: achanak shadeed dhamakta sar dard, chehra surkh hona, chot ki satah se upar bohat paseena, bohat aahista nabz, ya nazar dhundli hona. Yeh autonomic dysreflexia ho sakta hai, blood pressure ka jaan leva izafa, jo aksar bhare ya band bladder, band catheter, ya qabz se hota hai. Seedhay baith jayein, tang kapray dheele karein, agar aap ko aata ho to bladder khali karein ya catheter dekhein, aur fauran madad lein.
  • Aap peshab nahi kar sakte ya bladder khali nahi kar sakte, ya catheter band ho gaya hai ya nikal gaya hai aur wapis nahi dala ja sakta, dard ya sooje hue neechle pait ke saath.
  • Bukhar ya kapkapi, kamar ya pehlu mein dard, ya aap bohat bimar mehsoos karein.
  • Peshab mein khoon ke lothray ya zyada khoon.
  • Uljhan, shadeed kamzori, ya taangon mein achanak nayi kamzori ya sunn hona.

Meherbani karke, agar aap dhundla ya badboodar peshab, naya rissna, nayi takleef, ya apne bladder ke mamool mein tabdeeli dekhein to bhi jaldi doctor ko dikhayein. Emergency ki zaroorat nahi, lekin jaanch honi chahiye.

Aam sawalaat

Kya mera bladder kabhi dobara aam tarah kaam karega?

Yeh wajah aur nason ke nuqsan ki miqdar par munhasir hai. Kuch log bladder ka kaam wapis hasil kar lete hain aur kuch nahi. Dono soorat mein acha plan kidneys ko bachata hai aur zindagi aaramdeh rakhta hai.

Kya khud catheter dalna takleef-deh ya ghair mehfooz hai?

Tarbiyat aur saaf mamool ke saath yeh aam tor par aaramdeh aur mehfooz hota hai, aur zyada tar log thore arse mein isay aasan paate hain. Bohat se logon ke liye kidneys ko bachane ka yeh sab se kargar tareeqa hai. Nurse ya maahir aap ko ya aap ke dekh bhaal karne wale ko sikha sakta hai.

Jab main theek mehsoos karta hoon to scan aur tests kyun chahiye?

Kyunke bladder ke dabao, infection aur pathri se kidneys ko khamoshi se nuqsan ho sakta hai. Baqaida jaanch masail ko jaldi pakad leti hai.

Kya har peshab ke infection ko antibiotics chahiye?

Nahi. Peshab test theek mehsoos karne walon mein bhi jaraseem dikha sakta hai, aur is ka hamesha ilaaj zaroori nahi. Ilaaj tab karein jab bukhar, dard ya naya rissna jaisi alamaat hon, aur sirf doctor ke mashwaray se.

Kya main phir bhi aam samaji, kaam aur khandani zindagi guzar sakta hoon?

Ji haan. Neurogenic bladder wale bohat se log guzarte hain. Acha mamool, sahi saman aur sahara bara farq daalte hain. Agar aap ko qurbat ya fertility ki fikr ho to meherbani karke doctor se poochein.

Kya jari bootiyan ya khaas khuraak ilaaj hain?

Is ka koi acha saboot nahi ke yeh nason ke nuqsan ya bladder ke masail theek karte hain, aur kuch cheezein kidneys ko nuqsan pohancha sakti hain. Meherbani karke kisi nuskhay ke liye theek ilaaj na chhorein, aur jo kuch aap lete hain woh doctor ko batayein.

Mere bachay ko spina bifida hai. Kya yeh wahi hai?

Spina bifida wale bohat se bachon ko neurogenic bladder hota hai, aur wahi usool lagu hote hain: kidneys ko bachayein, infection se bachayein aur khushk rahein. Bachon ko bachon ke urologist aur taweel muddat ka plan chahiye. Dekhein vesicoureteric reflux guide.

Sab se ahem aik cheez jo main kar sakta hoon kya hai?

Follow-up visits aur apna bladder ka mamool jaari rakhein, kaafi peeyein, qabz se bachein, aur bukhar ya upar di gayi nishaniyon par jaldi madad lein.

What a neurogenic bladder is

The bladder is controlled by nerves that run between the bladder, the spinal cord and the brain. They tell you when the bladder is filling and let you empty it at the right time. When these nerves are damaged, the bladder may not empty properly, may squeeze at the wrong time, or both. Doctors call this a neurogenic bladder, or neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction.

It can lead to leaking, trouble passing urine, repeated urine infections, and, most importantly, damage to the kidneys if it is not looked after. The good news is that with the right plan most people stay well. This guide explains why it happens, why the kidneys matter, the main treatments, and the warning signs for urgent help. It is written for people with the condition and for their families and carers.

A neurogenic bladder needs a plan made with a specialist, and a routine that you keep. Please do not wait for symptoms to appear before seeing a urologist.

What causes it

Any condition that damages the brain, spinal cord or the nerves to the bladder can cause it. Common causes include:

  • Spinal cord injury, for example from a road accident or a fall from a height.
  • Stroke or brain injury.
  • Spina bifida and other conditions present from birth (see also the vesicoureteric reflux guide).
  • Multiple sclerosis, Parkinson disease and other nerve diseases.
  • Diabetes, which can damage the nerves over many years.
  • Surgery or radiotherapy in the pelvis, which can injure nerves near the bladder.
  • A slipped disc, a tumour of the spine, or an infection pressing on the spinal cord.

How the bladder is affected

Depending on where the nerves are damaged, the bladder may behave in different ways, and some people have a mixture:

  • An overactive bladder that squeezes suddenly and strongly, with sudden urges and leaking (see the overactive bladder guide).
  • An underactive bladder that does not squeeze well, so it fills too much and does not empty. The bladder becomes over-full and may dribble (see the urinary retention guide).
  • An outlet that does not relax when the bladder squeezes, so the bladder squeezes against a closed door, and the pressure builds up.

The pattern is found by tests. It decides the treatment, so please do not start tablets or catheter routines without a proper assessment.

Why it matters: the kidneys

When the bladder cannot empty or stays at high pressure, the pressure and the urine left behind can back up towards the kidneys and damage them over time, often without symptoms. Repeated infections and stones add to the risk. This is why the main aim of treatment is to keep the pressure in the bladder low and to keep it emptying well, so that the kidneys stay healthy, and then to keep you dry and comfortable.

Common signs

Signs vary widely, and some people feel almost nothing. They can include:

  • Leaking urine, with or without an urge.
  • Not being able to pass urine, or passing only a little, or a weak stream.
  • No feeling of the bladder filling, or a different feeling such as sweating, a pounding head or tummy discomfort.
  • Repeated urine infections, or fever without another cause.
  • Cloudy or smelly urine, or blood in the urine.
  • Stones in the bladder or kidney.

If you have a spinal injury or nerve disease, please do not rely on feeling alone. Regular checks matter because kidney damage can be silent.

How it is checked

A urologist, often with a specialist in rehabilitation, will usually:

  • Ask about the nerve problem, bladder and bowel habits, leaking, infections and medicines.
  • Examine you and check your nerves and skin.
  • Ask you to keep a bladder diary, and may ask you to record how much you pass.
  • Do urine and blood tests, including a check of how well your kidneys are working.
  • Do an ultrasound scan of the kidneys and bladder.
  • Do special pressure tests (urodynamics). A thin tube fills the bladder while pressures are recorded. This shows how the bladder behaves, and it is the key test for planning treatment.
  • Sometimes look inside the bladder with a camera, or arrange a kidney scan.

Treatment options

Treatment is individual. The usual goals are to protect the kidneys, avoid infections, and keep you dry. Options include:

Clean intermittent catheterisation (CIC)

Passing a thin, clean tube into the bladder several times a day to empty it fully. It is the most widely used and often the best method, because it empties the bladder fully, protects the kidneys, and avoids a permanent tube. It can be learned by most people, or done by a carer, with training. It needs clean hands and clean equipment, and a regular routine.

A long-term catheter

A tube that stays in, either through the urethra or through the tummy wall (a suprapubic catheter). It is used when intermittent catheterisation is not possible. It carries a higher risk of infections, blockage and stones, and needs regular changing and care.

Medicines to relax the bladder

For a bladder that squeezes too much, medicines can lower the pressure and reduce leaking. They may have side effects such as a dry mouth and constipation, and are often combined with catheterisation.

Botulinum toxin injections into the bladder

An injection that relaxes the bladder muscle for several months, then needs repeating. Many people need to catheterise afterwards.

Surgery

For a small number of people whose bladder remains at high pressure despite other treatment, an operation can enlarge the bladder or divert the urine. Surgery to the outlet, or to create a channel to the tummy wall for catheterisation, can also help. These are specialist operations.

The right treatment depends on the type of bladder, your hands and your lifestyle. A catheter routine only works well when it is started with proper training. Please do not copy another person's routine.

Urine infections and stones

Urine infections are common with a neurogenic bladder, especially with catheters. Not every positive urine test needs treatment, but fever, new leaking, new pain, a change in the smell or colour of the urine, or feeling unwell may mean an infection that does. Antibiotics should be taken only on a doctor's advice, and for the full course. Drinking enough, emptying the bladder fully and regularly, and keeping a clean routine reduce infections. Stones can form more easily too, so ask your doctor about checks. See the urine infection guide.

Bowels, skin and daily life

Nerve damage often affects the bowel as well, and constipation makes bladder problems worse, so a bowel routine is part of the plan. Take care of your skin, especially if you sit or lie for long periods, and keep it clean and dry to avoid sores. Aim to drink enough fluids through the day. Pads and aids can help during treatment. A rehabilitation team, occupational therapist and a family member who understands the routine can make a big difference. Many people with a neurogenic bladder work, study, marry, travel and live full lives.

Follow-up for life

A neurogenic bladder needs lifelong follow-up, because the bladder can change over time and kidney damage can be silent. Your doctor will usually repeat kidney and bladder scans, kidney blood tests and sometimes pressure tests, often once a year or as advised. Please do not skip these visits even when you feel well, and bring all your reports.

When to seek urgent help

Go to your nearest hospital emergency department straight away if you have any of the following:

  • If you have a spinal cord injury: a sudden severe pounding headache, flushing, heavy sweating above the level of the injury, a very slow pulse, or blurred vision. This can be autonomic dysreflexia, a life-threatening rise in blood pressure, often caused by a full or blocked bladder, a blocked catheter, or constipation. Sit upright, loosen tight clothing, empty the bladder or check the catheter if you know how, and get help at once.
  • You cannot pass urine or empty the bladder, or a catheter is blocked or has come out and cannot be replaced, with a painful or swollen lower tummy.
  • Fever or shaking chills, pain in the back or side, or you feel very unwell.
  • Blood clots or heavy bleeding in the urine.
  • Confusion, severe weakness, or sudden new leg weakness or numbness.

Please also see your doctor soon if you notice cloudy or smelly urine, new leaking, new discomfort, or a change in your bladder routine. It does not need the emergency department, but it should be checked.

Common questions

Will my bladder ever work normally again?

It depends on the cause and on how much nerve damage there is. Some people recover bladder function and some do not. Either way, a good plan protects the kidneys and keeps life comfortable.

Is catheterising myself painful or unsafe?

With training and a clean routine it is usually comfortable and safe, and most people find it easy after a short time. It is the most effective way for many people to protect the kidneys. A nurse or specialist can teach you or your carer.

Why do I need a scan and tests when I feel well?

Because the kidneys can be damaged silently by pressure in the bladder, and by infections and stones. Regular checks catch problems early.

Does every urine infection need antibiotics?

No. A urine test may show germs in people who feel well, and this does not always need treatment. Treat when there are symptoms such as fever, pain or new leaking, and only on a doctor's advice.

Can I still have a normal social, work and family life?

Yes. Many people with a neurogenic bladder do. A good routine, the right equipment, and support make a big difference. If you are worried about intimacy or fertility, please ask your doctor.

Are herbal remedies or special diets a cure?

There is no good evidence that they cure nerve damage or bladder problems, and some products can harm the kidneys. Please do not stop proper treatment for a remedy, and tell your doctor about everything you take.

My child has spina bifida. Is this the same?

Many children with spina bifida have a neurogenic bladder, and the same principles apply: protect the kidneys, avoid infections and stay dry. Children need a paediatric urologist and a long-term plan. See also the vesicoureteric reflux guide.

What is the single most important thing I can do?

Keep your follow-up visits and your bladder routine, drink enough, avoid constipation, and seek help early when you have a fever or the signs listed above.
