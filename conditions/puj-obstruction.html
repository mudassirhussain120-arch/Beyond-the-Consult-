Condition guide

PUJ obstruction (blockage where the kidney meets the ureter)PUJ obstruction (kidney aur ureter ke jorr par rukawat)

A PUJ obstruction is a narrowing where the kidney meets its drainage tube, so the kidney swells. It is often found before birth or in childhood. Many cases only need watching, and when an operation is needed it works well. This guide explains the signs, the tests, the options and when to seek urgent help.

PUJ obstruction woh tangi hai jahan kidney apni nikasi ki nali se milti hai, is liye kidney sooj jati hai. Yeh aksar paidaish se pehle ya bachpan mein pakri jati hai. Bohat se maamlaat mein sirf nigrani kaafi hoti hai, aur jab operation chahiye ho to acha kaam karta hai. Yeh guide nishaniyan, tests, intikhabat aur fauran madad kab lein, samjhati hai.

PUJ obstruction kya hai

Har kidney peshab ko darmiyan ke aik hisse mein jama karti hai jise renal pelvis kehte hain. Wahan se peshab aik patli nali, ureter, mein behta hai jo isay bladder tak le jati hai. Jahan pelvis ureter se milta hai use pelvi-ureteric junction (PUJ) kehte hain. PUJ obstruction mein yeh jorr tang ya band hota hai, is liye peshab aahista nikalta hai aur kidney sooj jati hai (isay hydronephrosis kehte hain).

Kuch PUJ obstruction halke hote hain aur nuqsan nahi karte. Doosre agar ilaaj na ho to kidney ko aahista nuqsan pohanchate hain. Dekh bhaal ka maqsad yeh maloom karna hai ke aap ya aap ke bachay ko kaun si qism hai. Yeh guide batati hai ke yeh kaise pakra jata hai, tests, kab sirf nigrani kaafi hai aur kab operation ka mashwara diya jata hai. Yeh bado aur walidain ke liye hai.

Scan par kidney ka soojha hona hamesha operation ki zaroorat nahi batata. Maahir bata sakta hai ke kidney kaafi acha bah rahi hai aur aam tarah kaam kar rahi hai ya nahi.

Yeh kaise pakri jati hai

PUJ obstruction bachon mein kidney ke sooj jane ki aam wajohat mein se aik hai, aur aksar hamal ke dauran scan mein pakri jati hai. Bade bachon aur baalighon mein yeh tab pakri jati hai jab peshab ka infection, pehlu ya kamar mein dard, kidney ki pathri, peshab mein khoon ho, ya kisi aur wajah se scan ho. Kabhi kabhi yeh bagair kisi alamat ke ittefaq se mil jati hai. Yeh aik kidney ya, kam aam tor par, dono ko mutasir kar sakti hai.

Yeh kyun hota hai

Aksar jorr paidaish se tang hota hai, ya to is liye ke deewar ka muscle theek kaam nahi karta ya ureter kidney se ghalat jagah milta hai. Kuch logon mein ureter ke samne se guzarti hui khoon ki nali us par dabao dalti hai. Baalighon mein yeh kidney ki pathri, infection, chot ya operation ke baad nishan ban jane se bhi ho sakta hai.

Yeh kisi aisi cheez se nahi hota jo maan ne hamal ke dauran ki ya na ki, aur yeh khuraak ya wazan uthane se nahi hota. Meherbani karke apne aap ko qasoorwar na thehrayen.

Aam nishaniyan

Bohat se log, aur bohat se bachay, koi alamat nahi rakhte. Jab hoti hain to yeh ho sakti hain:

  • Pehlu ya kamar mein dard, jo aata jata ho aur bohat pani peene ke baad zyada ho sakta hai.
  • Peshab ke infection, kabhi bukhar, ulti ya bimari ke ehsaas ke saath. Chhote bachon mein sirf bukhar hi nishani ho sakta hai.
  • Peshab mein khoon, kabhi halki chot ke baad.
  • Sooji hui kidney mein pathri ka banna.
  • Pehlu ya pait mein gaanth, jo zyada tar chhote bachon mein nazar aati hai.
  • Chhote bachon mein: doodh kam peena, kam barhna ya ulti.

Jaanch kaise hoti hai

Aap ke doctor aam tor par yeh karwayenge:

  • Ultrasound, jis se pata chalta hai ke kidney kitni soojhi hui hai aur doosri kidney theek hai ya nahi. Yeh mehfooz aur dard se paak hai, aur dohraya ja sakta hai.
  • Peshab ka test, infection dekhne ke liye.
  • Kidney ke kaam ka (renogram) scan, jis mein ragh mein bezarar nishan-dahi ka maada lagaya jata hai. Is se pata chalta hai ke har kidney kitna kaam kar rahi hai aur peshab theek nikalta hai ya nahi. Yeh operation ka faisla karne ka aham test hai.
  • Khoon ke tests, kidneys dekhne ke liye.
  • Kabhi kabhi CT ya MRI scan, zyada tar bade bachon aur baalighon mein, operation se pehle khoon ki nalion aur banawat dekhne ke liye.
  • Kabhi kabhi chhote bachon mein bladder aur ureters ka scan, reflux dekhne ke liye (dekhein vesicoureteric reflux guide).

Kab sirf nigrani kaafi hai

Har PUJ obstruction ko operation ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Bohat se bachon mein, aur kuch bade bachon aur baalighon mein, kidney acha kaam kar rahi hoti hai aur sojan halki hoti hai. In soorton mein maahir baqaida scans aur peshab ke tests ke saath nigrani ka mashwara de sakta hai, kyunke bohat se bachay waqt ke saath behtar ho jate hain. Isay watchful waiting kehte hain. Yeh tay shuda tareeqa hai, kuch na karna nahi.

Watchful waiting tab hi kaam karta hai jab follow-up scans kiye jayein. Agar kidney ka kaam kam ho, sojan barhe, infection shuru hon ya dard ho to plan operation mein badal sakta hai. Meherbani karke yeh appointments na chhorein.

Ilaaj ke tareeqay

Operation ka mashwara tab diya jata hai jab kidney kaafi acha kaam ya nikasi nahi kar rahi, bigar rahi ho, ya dard, infection ya pathri ka sabab ban rahi ho. Bunyadi intikhabat:

Pyeloplasty

Yeh aam operation hai. Jorr ka tang hissa nikal diya jata hai aur sehatmand ureter ko dobara kidney se jora jata hai taake peshab aasani se behta rahe. Yeh pehlu mein khule cheere se, keyhole se, ya robot ki madad se keyhole se ho sakta hai, mareez ki umar aur surgeon ke tajarbe ke hisab se. Yeh general anaesthetic mein hota hai. Aksar kuch arse ke liye andar aik patli nali (stent) chhodi jati hai jo bharne mein madad deti hai, aur baad mein chhote camera amal se nikal li jati hai.

Jorr ko andar se kholna (endoscopic ilaaj)

Kuch baalighon mein tang hissa patli doorbeen se ureter ke raste andar se khola ja sakta hai, aur baad mein stent chhodi jati hai. Yeh chuninda soorton mein istemal hota hai aur aam qism ki rukawat ke liye pyeloplasty ke muqable mein kam kamyab hota hai.

Aarzi nikasi

Agar shadeed infection ho ya kidney bohat kharab nikal rahi ho to peeth ki jild ke zariye kidney mein nali (nephrostomy) ya andar stent rakhi ja sakti hai, jab tak infection theek na ho aur pakka ilaaj tay na ho.

Kidney nikalna, shaz o nadir

Agar kidney itni kharab ho gayi ho ke theek na ho sake, masail paida kar rahi ho aur doosri kidney sehatmand ho to kabhi kabhi kidney nikalni pad sakti hai. Yeh nadir hai, aur sirf kidney ke kaam ke scan ke baad tay hota hai.

Pyeloplasty zyada tar logon mein acha kaam karta hai. Aap ke surgeon khatre samjhayenge, jin mein infection, khoon behna, stent ki wajah se takleef aur kabhi tangi ka wapis aana shamil hai. Meherbani karke haan kehne se pehle koi bhi sawal poochein.

Operation ke baad

Zyada tar log thore arse ke liye hospital mein rehte hain. Chand din catheter aur drain istemal ho sakte hain. Agar stent rakhi gayi ho to baad mein nikal di jayegi. Jab tak andar ho, thori jaldi, takleef ya peshab mein khoon ho sakta hai. Baad mein scans dohraye jate hain, aksar kai mahine baad, taake dekha jaye ke kidney theek nikal rahi hai. Bachon mein follow-up kuch arse tak jaari reh sakta hai, kyunke kidney barhti rehti hai. Meherbani karke theek mehsoos karne par bhi yeh visits na chhorein.

Agar aap ke bachay ko bataya gaya ho

Paidaish se pehle yeh pata chalna ke bachay ki kidney soojhi hui hai fikr ki baat hai. Meherbani karke yaad rakhein ke bohat se bachon mein sojan halki hoti hai aur kidney ko nuqsan nahi pohanchati, aur plan aam tor par paidaish ke baad scans aur baqaida follow-up hota hai. Aap ke bachay ke doctor batayenge ke kya zaroori hai. Shuru ke mahino mein kabhi infection se bachao ke liye antibiotics ka aik ehtiyati course tajweez kiya jata hai, aur doctor faisla karenge ke yeh aap ke bachay par lagu hota hai ya nahi. Meherbani karke scans ki tamam appointments rakhein, aur bukhar ho to bachay ko jaldi doctor ko dikhayein, kyunke peshab ka infection bunyadi khatra hai. Dekhein urine infection guide.

Fauran madad kab lein

Agar in mein se koi bhi ho to fauran apne qareebi hospital ke emergency department jayein:

  • Bukhar ya kapkapi ke saath pehlu ya kamar mein dard, ya ulti.
  • Bukhar wala bachcha jo bimar ho, doodh ya pani na pi raha ho, ya bohat sust ho.
  • Pehlu mein shadeed dard jo kam na ho, khaas tor par ulti ke saath.
  • Bohat kam ya bilkul peshab na aana, khaas tor par agar sirf aik kaam karne wali kidney ho.
  • Operation ke baad: bukhar, shadeed dard, zyada khoon, ya zakhm jo surkh, soojha hua ho ya jis se pani rissta ho.

Meherbani karke, agar peshab mein khoon, pehlu mein nayi dard, ya baar baar peshab ke infection hon to bhi intezar kiye baghair jaldi doctor ko dikhayein. Emergency ki zaroorat nahi, lekin jaanch honi chahiye.

Aam sawalaat

Kya soojhi hui kidney ko hamesha operation chahiye?

Nahi. Bohat si halki hoti hain aur scans ke saath dekhi jati hain. Operation tab tajweez hota hai jab kidney kaafi acha kaam ya nikasi nahi kar rahi, ya bigar rahi hai ya masail paida kar rahi hai.

Kya operation ke baad mere bachay ki kidney theek ho jayegi?

Zyada tar bachon mein pyeloplasty ke baad kidney behtar hoti hai ya wesi hi rehti hai, aur sojan waqt ke saath kam hoti hai. Kitna kaam wapis aata hai yeh is par hai ke operation se pehle kitna nuqsan tha. Aap ke doctor samjhayenge.

Kya chhote bachay ya bachay ke liye operation khatarnak hai?

Pyeloplasty aik mustahkam operation hai jo bachon ke tajarbe-kaar teams karti hain. Har operation mein khatre hote hain, aur surgeon samjhayenge. Meherbani karke koi bhi sawal poochein.

Kya PUJ obstruction ka ilaaj goliyon se ho sakta hai?

Nahi. Goliyan tang jorr ko nahi kholti. Yeh sirf infection ya dard ke liye istemal hoti hain.

Kya operation ke baad yeh wapis aa sakta hai?

Kabhi kabhi tangi wapis aa jati hai. Isi liye follow-up scans kiye jate hain. Agar aaye to aam tor par dobara ilaaj ho sakta hai.

Kya main aik kidney ke saath normal zindagi guzar sakta hoon?

Ji haan. Bohat se log aik kidney ke saath sehatmand zindagi guzarte hain, bashartay ke us ki baqaida jaanch ho aur use infection aur pathri se bachaya jaye.

Kya yeh hamare kisi kaam ki wajah se hua?

Nahi. Yeh aam tor par paidaish se hota hai, aur walidain ya mareez ke kisi kaam se nahi hota.

Kya baalighon ko bhi PUJ obstruction ho sakta hai?

Ji haan. Yeh bachpan se ho kar baad mein pakra ja sakta hai, ya pathri, chot ya nishan ke baad ho sakta hai. Ilaaj milta julta hai.

What PUJ obstruction is

Each kidney collects urine in a central area called the renal pelvis. From there, urine drains into a thin tube, the ureter, which carries it to the bladder. The place where the pelvis joins the ureter is the pelvi-ureteric junction (PUJ). In PUJ obstruction, this junction is narrow or blocked, so urine drains slowly and the kidney swells (this is called hydronephrosis).

Some PUJ obstructions are mild and cause no harm. Others slowly damage the kidney if they are not treated. The aim of care is to find out which type you or your child has. This guide explains how it is found, the tests, when it can be watched and when an operation is advised. It is written for adults and for parents.

A swollen kidney on a scan does not always mean that an operation is needed. A specialist can tell whether the kidney is draining well enough and working normally.

How it is found

PUJ obstruction is one of the more common reasons for a swollen kidney in babies, and it is often found on a scan during pregnancy. In older children and adults it is found when someone has a urine infection, pain in the side or back, kidney stones, blood in the urine, or when a scan is done for another reason. It is sometimes found by chance in someone with no symptoms at all. It can affect one kidney or, less often, both.

Why it happens

Most often, the junction has been narrow since birth, either because the muscle in the wall does not work properly or because the ureter joins the kidney in an awkward place. In some people, a blood vessel crossing in front of the ureter presses on it. In adults it can also follow a kidney stone, infection, injury or an operation, which leave scarring.

It is not caused by anything a mother did or did not do during pregnancy, and it is not caused by diet or lifting. Please do not blame yourself.

Common signs

Many people, and many babies, have no symptoms. When they do appear, they can include:

  • Pain in the side or back, which may come and go and may be worse after drinking a lot of fluid.
  • Urine infections, sometimes with fever, vomiting or feeling unwell. In babies, a fever may be the only sign.
  • Blood in the urine, sometimes after a minor injury.
  • Kidney stones forming in the swollen kidney.
  • A lump in the side or tummy, which is seen mainly in babies.
  • In young children: poor feeding, poor growth or vomiting.

How it is checked

Your doctor will usually arrange:

  • An ultrasound scan, which shows how swollen the kidney is and whether the other kidney is normal. It is safe and painless, and it can be repeated.
  • A urine test, to look for infection.
  • A kidney function (renogram) scan, using a harmless tracer injected into a vein. It shows how much each kidney is working, and whether it drains well. This is the key test when deciding whether an operation is needed.
  • Blood tests, to check the kidneys.
  • Sometimes a CT or MRI scan, mainly in older children and adults, to see the blood vessels and the anatomy before surgery.
  • Sometimes a scan of the bladder and ureters in babies, to check for reflux (see the vesicoureteric reflux guide).

When it can be watched

Not every PUJ obstruction needs an operation. In many babies, and in some older children and adults, the kidney is working well and the swelling is mild. In these cases the specialist may advise watching, with regular scans and urine tests, because many babies improve with time. This is called watchful waiting. It is a planned approach, not doing nothing.

Watchful waiting only works if the follow-up scans are kept. If the kidney starts to work less well, the swelling increases, infections begin, or pain develops, the plan may change to an operation. Please do not miss these appointments.

Treatment options

An operation is advised when the kidney is not draining or working well enough, when it is getting worse, or when it causes pain, infections or stones. The main options are:

Pyeloplasty

This is the standard operation. The narrow part of the junction is removed, and the healthy ureter is joined again to the kidney so that urine can drain freely. It can be done by an open cut in the side, by keyhole surgery, or by robot-assisted keyhole surgery, depending on the age of the patient and the surgeon's experience. It is done under general anaesthetic. A thin tube (a stent) is often left inside for some time to support the healing, and is later removed through a short camera procedure.

Opening the junction from inside (endoscopic treatment)

In some adults, the narrow part can be opened from inside using a thin telescope passed up the ureter, with a stent left in afterwards. It is used in selected cases and tends to be less successful than pyeloplasty for the usual type of blockage.

Temporary drainage

If there is a serious infection or a very poorly draining kidney, a tube may be placed into the kidney through the skin of the back (nephrostomy) or a stent placed inside, to drain it until the infection settles and definite treatment is planned.

Removing a kidney, rarely

If a kidney has been damaged beyond recovery and is causing problems, and the other kidney is healthy, a kidney may occasionally need to be removed. This is rare, and it is decided only after a kidney function scan.

Pyeloplasty works well in most people. Your surgeon will explain the risks, which include infection, bleeding, a stent that causes discomfort and, occasionally, the narrowing returning. Please ask any questions before you agree.

After the operation

Most people stay in hospital for a short time. A catheter and a drain may be used for a few days. If a stent was placed, it will be taken out later. It can cause some urgency, discomfort or blood in the urine while it is in. Scans are repeated afterwards, often several months later, to check that the kidney is draining well. In children the follow-up may continue for some time, because the kidney keeps growing. Please attend these visits even if you or your child feels well.

If your baby has been told about it

Finding out before birth that a baby's kidney is swollen is worrying. Please remember that in many babies the swelling is mild and does not harm the kidney, and the plan is usually scans after birth and regular follow-up. Your baby's doctor will explain what is needed. A preventive course of antibiotics is sometimes advised in the early months, and your doctor will decide whether that applies to your baby. Please keep all scan appointments, and take your baby to a doctor promptly if there is a fever, because urine infection is the main risk. See the urine infection guide.

When to seek urgent help

Go to your nearest hospital emergency department straight away if you have any of the following:

  • Fever or shaking chills with pain in the side or back, or vomiting.
  • A baby or child with a fever who is unwell, not feeding or drinking, or very sleepy.
  • Severe pain in the side that does not settle, especially with vomiting.
  • Passing very little or no urine, particularly if you have only one working kidney.
  • After an operation: fever, severe pain, heavy bleeding, or a wound that is red, swollen or leaking.

Please also see a doctor soon, without waiting, if you notice blood in the urine, new side pain, or repeated urine infections. It does not need the emergency department, but it should be checked.

Common questions

Does a swollen kidney always need an operation?

No. Many are mild and are watched with scans. An operation is advised only if the kidney is not working or draining well enough, or if it is getting worse or causing problems.

Will my child's kidney recover after the operation?

In most children the kidney improves or stays stable after pyeloplasty, and the swelling reduces over time. How much function returns depends on how much damage there was before the operation. Your doctor will explain.

Is the operation dangerous for a baby or child?

Pyeloplasty is a well-established operation done by teams experienced in children. Every operation has risks, and your surgeon will explain them. Please ask any questions.

Can PUJ obstruction be treated with tablets?

No. Tablets do not open a narrow junction. They are used only for infection or pain.

Will it come back after surgery?

Occasionally the narrowing returns. That is why follow-up scans are done. If it does, it can usually be treated again.

Can I live normally with one kidney?

Yes. Many people live healthy lives with one kidney, as long as it is checked regularly and protected from infections and stones.

Is it caused by anything we did?

No. It is usually present from birth, and nothing that a parent or the person did causes it.

Can adults have PUJ obstruction?

Yes. It may be present since childhood and found later, or it may follow stones, injury or scarring. Treatment is similar.
