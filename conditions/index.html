Conditions

Understanding your diagnosis.

Each guide explains what a condition is, how common it is, its usual symptoms, how it's diagnosed, the treatment options, follow-up, and when to seek urgent help.

  • Kidney and ureteric stones

    Hard deposits that form in the kidney. They can move into the ureter, the tube to the bladder, and cause sudden, severe pain.

    Read the guide

  • Enlarged prostate (BPH)

    A common, non-cancerous enlargement of the prostate in older men, which can make passing urine slower, weaker or more frequent.

    Read the guide

  • Urinary tract infection (UTI)

    An infection in the bladder, or sometimes the kidneys. Common and usually easy to treat, but a UTI with fever needs prompt care.

    Read the guide

  • Urinary retention (bladder not emptying)

    When the bladder cannot empty properly. It can come on suddenly and painfully, which is an emergency, or build up slowly without pain.

    Read the guide

  • Prostate cancer

    A cancer that starts in the prostate gland. Often slow-growing and often without symptoms early on. How it is found, how it is checked, and the main treatment choices.

    Read the guide

  • Bladder cancer

    A cancer that starts in the lining of the bladder. Often first seen as painless blood in the urine. How it is found, the main treatments, and why regular check-ups matter.

    Read the guide

  • Kidney cancer

    A cancer that starts in the kidney. Often found by chance on a scan. How it is found, the stages in plain words, the treatment options, and living well with one kidney.

    Read the guide

  • Testicular cancer

    A cancer that usually shows as a lump in one testicle, often painless. Very often curable. How to check yourself, how it is found, the treatment options and protecting fertility.

    Read the guide

  • Testicular torsion (twisted testicle)

    A medical emergency. Sudden, severe testicle pain needs hospital care straight away to save the testicle. The signs, what happens in hospital, and where to go.

    Read the guide

  • Erectile dysfunction (ED)

    Trouble getting or keeping an erection. Common, usually has findable causes, and can very often be helped. Why it happens, how it is checked, and the treatment options.

    Read the guide

  • Hydrocele (fluid swelling around the testicle)

    A painless swelling of the scrotum caused by fluid around the testicle. Usually harmless, but it needs a proper check. Causes, treatment options and when to seek help.

    Read the guide

  • Varicocele (enlarged veins in the scrotum)

    Swollen veins around the testicle, like varicose veins. Common and often harmless, but it can cause discomfort or affect fertility. How it is checked, who needs treatment, and the options.

    Read the guide

  • Male infertility (difficulty fathering a child)

    When a couple has not conceived and the man's sperm may be part of the reason. The causes, how it is checked, the treatment options, and what to expect, without false promises.

    Read the guide

  • Epididymo-orchitis (infection of the testicle and its tube)

    An infection that makes the testicle and the tube behind it painful and swollen. Usually treatable, but sudden severe pain can mean a twisted testicle. Causes, treatment and when to seek help.

    Read the guide
