Symptom guide

Blood in semen (haematospermia)Semen mein khoon (haematospermia)

Seeing blood in your semen can be frightening. In most men it is not caused by anything serious, and often it settles by itself. But it should be checked, particularly if it keeps coming back or you are older. This guide explains the common causes, how doctors find the reason, what to do next, and when to go to hospital.

Apne semen mein khoon dekhna ghabra dene wala ho sakta hai. Zyadatar mardon mein yeh kisi sanjeeda wajah se nahi hota, aur aksar khud theek ho jata hai. Lekin is ki jaanch honi chahiye, khaas tor par agar yeh baar baar ho ya aap ki umar zyada ho. Yeh guide aam wajohat, doctor wajah kaise dhoondte hain, ab kya karein, aur kab hospital jayein, samjhati hai.

Yeh alamat kya hai

Semen mein khoon (haematospermia) ka matlab hai ke semen gulabi, surkh, bhura nazar aaye, ya is mein lakeeren ya lothray khoon ke hon. Yeh aam tor par ejaculation par nazar aata hai, aur bilkul dard ke baghair ho sakta hai. Purana khoon bhura ya zang jaisa nazar aata hai aur jima mein waqfay ke baad aam hai.

Bohat se mard fikr karte hain ke yeh cancer ya jinsi infection ka matlab hai, aur isay chhupa lete hain. Meherbani karke aisa na karein. Yeh aam tor par beneqsan hota hai, aur doctor se jaldi baat karne se tasalli milti hai.

Aam wajohat

  • Soojan ya infection prostate, seminal vesicles ya peshab ki nali ki. Is ke saath ejaculation par dard, peshab mein jalan, pelvis mein dard, ya discharge ho sakta hai (dekhein penis ya ejaculation ke dard ki guide).
  • Jinsi taluq se lagne wala infection. Is se semen mein khoon aa sakta hai aur yeh khamosh bhi ho sakta hai. Test raazdari se hota hai, aur saathi ko bhi ilaaj chahiye.
  • Prostate ki biopsy ya doosre procedure ke baad. Is ke baad kuch hafton tak semen mein khoon aam hai aur aksar khud theek ho jata hai. Aap ke urologist batayenge ke kya umeed rakhein.
  • Chot ya tanaao, maslan groin par waar, bhari wazan uthana, ya ejaculation mein lambe waqfay ke baad jima.
  • Blood pressure ki zyadati, ya khoon patla karne wali dawaiyan, jin se khoon behne ka imkaan barhta hai. Meherbani karke koi dawai khud na chhorein. Pehle apne doctor se baat karein.
  • Barhi hui prostate (BPH) ya ghudood mein chhoti pathri ya cyst (dekhein enlarged prostate guide).
  • Peshab ya jinsi nizam ki TB kam aam wajah hai, aur un mardon mein zyada hai jinhein TB ho chuki ho ya jahan yeh aam ho.
  • Cancer prostate, bladder ya testicle ka kam aam wajah hai, lekin jaanch ka mashwara isi liye diya jata hai, khaas tor par 40 se oopar ke mardon ke liye ya jab khoon baar baar aaye (dekhein prostate cancer guide).
  • Koi wajah nahi milti. Bohat se mardon mein, khaas tor par naujawanon mein aik baar ke daure mein, tests normal hote hain aur khoon dobara nahi aata.

Jaanch aam tor par kaise hoti hai

Aap ke doctor poochhenge ke yeh kitni baar hua, kya dard, jalan, bukhar, peshab mein khoon, haali procedure ya chot hai, aap ki umar, aur koi dawaiyan ya pehle TB. Woh aap ka blood pressure dekhenge, aur penis, groin, scrotum aur aam tor par prostate ka muaina karenge. Aam tests yeh hain:

  • Peshab ka test, infection aur khoon ke liye.
  • Swab ya pehle peshab ka sample, agar jinsi taluq se lagne wale infection ka imkaan ho.
  • Khoon ka test, jis mein khoon jamne ka jaiza bhi shamil hai agar aap ko aasani se khoon behta ho.
  • PSA khoon ka test, kuch mardon mein, umar aur khatre ke mutabiq, is baat par guftagu ke baad ke result kya bata sakta hai aur kya nahi (guide jald aa rahi hai).
  • Ultrasound, prostate, seminal vesicles, scrotum aur kidneys ka.
  • Prostate ka MRI scan agar khoon baar baar aaye ya doosre tests wazeh na hon (guide jald aa rahi hai).
  • Bladder aur peshab ki nali ka camera test, agar peshab mein bhi khoon ho ya wajah na mile.

Fauran madad kab lein

Agar semen mein khoon ho aur in mein se koi bhi ho to fauran qareebi hospital ke emergency department jayein:

  • Aap peshab nahi kar pa rahe (dekhein peshab na aane ki guide).
  • Bukhar ya kapkapi pelvis, scrotum ke peechay ya peshab karte waqt dard ke saath. Infected prostate sanjeeda ho sakti hai.
  • Zyada khoon behna, peshab mein lothray, ya chakkar, kamzori ya behoshi mehsoos karna.
  • Kisi testicle mein achanak shadeed dard (dekhein scrotum mein dard ya soojan ki guide).
  • Khoon jo band na ho haali biopsy ya procedure ke baad.

Semen mein khoon ka aik daura in mein se kisi ke baghair emergency department ki zaroorat nahi rakhta, lekin meherbani karke doctor se jaanch karwayein. Agar yeh baar baar aaye, ya peshab mein bhi khoon ho, to meherbani karke jald urologist ko dikhayein.

Ab kya karein

  • Aise doctor ko dikhayein jis par aap bharosa karte hain, aur saaf batayein ke aap ne kya dekha aur kitni baar. Aap tanhai mein dikhane ke liye keh sakte hain.
  • Andaaz note karein. Kitni baar hua, rang kaisa tha, aur kya dard, jalan ya haali procedure thi. Chhota sa note aap ke doctor ki madad karta hai.
  • Medical store se antibiotics na kharidein, aur jari booti, hakeem ya jinsi taqat ki cheezein istemal na karein. Yeh infection chhupa sakti hain, sahi ilaaj mein deri kar sakti hain aur kabhi khoon behne ka sabab banti hain.
  • Likhi hui dawaiyan khud na chhorein, blood pressure ki goliyan ya khoon patla karne wali dawaiyan samait. Pehle doctor se poochhein.
  • Dikhane aur ilaaj hone tak jinsi taluq se parhez karein, aur agar karein to condom istemal karein. Agar infection nikle to poochhein ke saathi ka bhi muaina hona chahiye.
  • Har qism ka tambaku chhor dein. Tambaku peenay aur chabane se bladder aur doosre cancers ka khatra barhta hai, aur chhorna har wajah mein aap ki sehat ke liye achha hai.
  • Din bhar paani peeyein aur bhari wazan uthane aur zor lagane se bachein jab tak yeh theek ho raha ho.
  • Urologist ko dikhayein agar yeh aik se zyada baar ho, aap 40 se oopar hon, peshab mein khoon ho, ya dard, bukhar ya gaanth ho.

Aam sawalaat

Kya semen mein khoon cancer ki nishani hai?

Kam hi. Zyadatar mardon mein beneqsan wajah hoti hai ya koi nahi milti. Isi liye doctor jaanch karte hain, khaas tor par agar aap 40 se oopar hon, khoon baar baar aaye, ya peshab mein bhi khoon ho.

Kya iska matlab hai ke mujhe jinsi taluq se lagne wala infection hai?

Hamesha nahi, lekin ho sakta hai. Yeh infections aam hain aur khamosh ho sakte hain, is liye waqt se kuch sabit nahi hota. Aap se raazdari se aur bina faislay ke poochha jayega kyunke is se ilaaj badalta hai. Saathi ko bhi ilaaj ki zaroorat ho sakti hai.

Yeh sirf aik baar hua aur main theek mehsoos kar raha hoon. Kya phir bhi doctor ko dikhana zaroori hai?

Jaanch munasib hai, chahe mukhtasar ho, kyunke is se aap ki fikr door hoti hai aur woh chand wajohat milti hain jinhein ilaaj chahiye. Naujawan mard mein aik baar ka daura jis ki jaanch normal ho aksar mazeed kuch nahi chahta.

Kya masturbation ya zyada jima se aisa hota hai?

Nahi. Yeh myths hain aur in se bimari nahi hoti. Lekin lambe waqfay ke baad, ya sakht ya zor ki sargarmi ke baad, thora purana khoon aa sakta hai jo khatarnak nahi hota.

Mujhe prostate ki biopsy hui thi. Kya semen mein khoon aam hai?

Haan. Biopsy ke baad kuch hafton tak yeh aam hai aur aksar khud theek ho jata hai. Meherbani karke apne urologist ko batayein agar yeh bohat zyada ho, bukhar ho, ya aap peshab na kar sakein.

Kya is se meri fertility par asar parega?

Aam tor par nahi. Lekin kuch wajohat, jaise prostate ka infection, sperm ko mutasir kar sakti hain, aur ilaaj se madad milti hai. Agar aap aur aap ki biwi bachay ki koshish kar rahe hain to meherbani karke batayein. Dekhein bachay ke hone mein mushkil ki guide.

Kya ghar par ise rokne ke liye kuch kar sakta hoon?

Haqeeqatan nahi. Aaram, paani peena aur zor lagane se bachna dikhane tak madad karte hain, lekin wajah ki sahi jaanch zaroori hai. Meherbani karke jari booti ya medical store ke nuskhay istemal na karein.

What this symptom is

Blood in the semen (haematospermia) means the semen looks pink, red, brown or has streaks or clots of blood. It is usually seen at ejaculation, and may come with no pain at all. Old blood looks brown or rusty and is common after a gap in sex.

Many men are worried that it means cancer or a sexual infection, and keep it secret. Please do not. It is usually harmless, and speaking to a doctor early brings peace of mind.

Common causes

  • Inflammation or infection of the prostate, the seminal vesicles or the urine tube. It may come with pain on ejaculation, burning when passing urine, an ache in the pelvis, or discharge (see the penile pain or pain on ejaculation guide).
  • An infection passed on by sexual contact. It can cause blood in the semen and may also be silent. Testing is private, and partners need treatment too.
  • After a prostate biopsy or other procedure. Blood in the semen is common for some weeks afterwards and usually settles by itself. Your urologist will tell you what to expect.
  • An injury or strain, such as a blow to the groin, heavy lifting, or a long gap between ejaculations followed by sex.
  • Raised blood pressure, or medicines that thin the blood, which make bleeding more likely. Please do not stop any medicine on your own. Speak to your doctor first.
  • An enlarged prostate (BPH) or a small stone or cyst in the glands (see the enlarged prostate guide).
  • Tuberculosis of the urinary or genital tract is a rare cause, and is more likely in men who have had TB or live where it is common.
  • Cancer of the prostate, bladder or testicle is a rare cause, but it is the reason a check is advised, especially for men over 40 or when the bleeding keeps coming back (see the prostate cancer guide).
  • No cause found. In many men, particularly younger men with a single episode, tests are normal and the bleeding does not return.

How it is usually investigated

Your doctor will ask how often it has happened, whether there is pain, burning, fever, blood in the urine, a recent procedure or injury, your age, and any medicines or past TB. They will check your blood pressure, and examine the penis, groin, scrotum and usually the prostate. Usual tests are:

  • A urine test, for infection and blood.
  • A swab or first-catch urine sample, if an infection passed on by sexual contact is possible.
  • A blood test, including a check of blood clotting if you bleed easily.
  • A PSA blood test, in some men, depending on age and risk, after a discussion of what the result can and cannot tell you (see the PSA test guide).
  • An ultrasound scan, of the prostate, seminal vesicles, scrotum and kidneys.
  • An MRI scan of the prostate if the bleeding keeps coming back or other tests are not clear (see the MRI of the prostate guide).
  • A camera test of the bladder and urine tube, if there is also blood in the urine or the cause is not found.

When to seek urgent help

Go to the nearest hospital emergency department if you have blood in the semen and any of these:

  • You cannot pass urine (see the unable to pass urine guide).
  • Fever or shaking chills with pain in the pelvis, behind the scrotum or on passing urine. An infected prostate can become serious.
  • Heavy bleeding, clots in the urine, or feeling faint, dizzy or very weak.
  • Sudden severe pain in a testicle (see the scrotal pain or swelling guide).
  • Bleeding that does not stop after a recent biopsy or procedure.

A single episode of blood in the semen without any of these does not need the emergency department, but please arrange a check with a doctor. If it keeps coming back, or you also have blood in the urine, please see a urologist soon.

What to do next

  • See a doctor you trust, and say plainly what you have seen and how often. You can ask to be seen in private.
  • Note the pattern. How many times it has happened, the colour, and whether there was pain, burning or a recent procedure. A short note helps your doctor.
  • Do not buy antibiotics from a counter, or use herbal, hakeem or sexual-strength products. They can hide an infection, delay the right treatment and sometimes cause bleeding.
  • Do not stop prescribed medicines on your own, including blood pressure tablets or blood thinners. Ask your doctor first.
  • Avoid sex until you have been seen and treated, and use a condom if you do have sex. If an infection is found, ask whether your partner should also be checked.
  • Stop all tobacco. Smoking and chewing tobacco raise the risk of bladder and other cancers, and quitting helps your health whatever the cause.
  • Drink water through the day and avoid heavy lifting and straining while it is settling.
  • See a urologist if it happens more than once, if you are over 40, if there is blood in the urine, or if there is pain, fever, or a lump.

Common questions

Is blood in the semen a sign of cancer?

Rarely. Most men have a harmless cause or none is found. That is why a doctor checks, particularly if you are over 40, if the bleeding keeps coming back, or if there is also blood in the urine.

Does it mean I have a sexually passed infection?

Not always, but it can. These infections are common and can be silent, so timing proves nothing. You will be asked in private and without judgement because it changes the treatment. Partners may also need treatment.

It happened only once and I feel well. Do I still need to see a doctor?

A check is sensible, even if it is brief, because it settles your worry and finds the few causes that need treatment. A single episode in a young man with a normal check often needs nothing more.

Does masturbation or too much sex cause it?

No. These are myths and do not cause illness. But after a long gap, or after rough or vigorous activity, a small amount of old blood can appear and is not dangerous.

I had a prostate biopsy. Is blood in the semen normal?

Yes. It is common for some weeks after a biopsy and usually settles by itself. Please call your urologist if it is heavy, if you have fever, or if you cannot pass urine.

Will it affect my fertility?

Usually not. But some causes, such as infection of the prostate, can affect sperm, and treating them helps. If you and your wife are trying for a child, please mention it. See the difficulty conceiving guide.

Is there anything I can do at home to stop it?

Not really. Resting, drinking water and avoiding straining help while you wait to be seen, but the cause needs a proper check. Please do not use herbal or counter remedies.
